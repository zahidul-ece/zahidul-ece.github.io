## Course Overview
ECE 587 provides a comprehensive introduction to modern techniques for secure and efficient operation of power systems. The course covers generation, transmission, and distribution systems, while introducing optimization methods central to real-world practice, including **economic dispatch (ED), unit commitment (UC), and optimal power flow (OPF)**. 

Students also explore **power system security, reliability, and uncertainty**, **load and price forecasting**, the role of **distributed energy resources (DERs) and microgrids**, and the increasing importance of **cyber networks** in power system operation. A forward-looking component addresses **AI applications**, including deep learning and large language models, in smart grid operation and monitoring.
